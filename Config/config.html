Your own media

For the titles Jellyfin found on disk.

When enabled, Gelato will inject missing seasons and episodes (as virtual items) into locally scanned shows, so you can stream episodes you don't have on disk. Run the Sync Series Trees task after changing this setting — it will create virtual items when enabled, or remove them when disabled.

Catalogs

Enable Stremio catalogs to import them into Jellyfin. Sequential Import: Series are processed one-by-one to respect rate limits.

Unreleased titles are imported but stay hidden while Filter unreleased items is on (Search tab).

Addon search

A Jellyfin search also asks AIOStreams, and what it finds can be opened and played straight away. Who searches only the library can be set per user on the Users tab.

A Jellyfin search also finds what is not in your libraries yet. Off: searches find only what is in your libraries already, for every user, and nothing is asked of AIOStreams or TMDB.
An actor's or director's page also lists their other films and series from TMDB, and they open like search results. Only while the search above is on.
Opening a search result still adds it to the library, but it stays out of the library, home rows and counts until someone plays it, marks it a favourite, likes it (a watchlist) or adds it to a collection or playlist. Search still finds it. Titles nobody played are removed again after 5 days.

Search bar toggle

Adds a local search toggle to the searchbar. Requires Jellyfin-JavaScript-Injector plugin to be installed and configured.

Unreleased titles

Keeps titles that are not released yet out of search results, and hides them in your Gelato libraries until they are.

Items released within these many days will be hidden if filter is enabled.

Streams

Probes a stream when you open its page or pick another version, and the next episode while one is ending, so playback starts without waiting for the probe. The next episode's stream is the one of the same binge group as the stream that is playing, when the addon sets one.
A stream known not to work (a dead link, or one with no video) is skipped for the next stream of the title, up to three. A stream that works starts as fast as before. A version you picked yourself plays as it is.
Cache duration for external stream URLs.

RemuxDB

Shows audio, subtitles and quality of a stream before you play it, and starts playback faster.
Shares this info for streams you play, so others see it too. Anonymous: no links, accounts or personal data.

FFmpeg
