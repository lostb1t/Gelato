Gelato

AIOStreams

Everything Gelato shows comes from your AIOStreams addon: its catalogs, its search, and its streams.

The manifest URL AIOStreams shows under Install, with or without /manifest.json.

Where titles go

Where the titles you open from search or import go. A catalog can have a library of its own on the Catalogs tab.

Where movies go unless a catalog has its own library. Pick any of your movie libraries, also one with your own media in it, or New library to get a folder for a new one.
Where series go unless a catalog has its own library. Pick any of your shows libraries, also one with your own media in it, or New library to get a folder for a new one.
Where Gelato creates the folders it adds to your libraries. Leave it empty for the default shown.

Your own media

For titles Jellyfin found on disk.

Movies and episodes Jellyfin found on disk also get the addon's streams, as more versions.
Your own series also list the seasons and episodes you don't have on disk, and you can stream them. When you save a change, the Sync series trees task starts and adds them, or removes them when this is off.

Images

Downloads images when they're first shown instead of at import, which saves a lot of storage.

Requests from Seerr

A request approved in Seerr (Jellyseerr, Overseerr) adds the title to your library. In Seerr, go to Settings > Notifications > Webhook and fill in these fields.

This server's address as your browser reaches it. If Seerr reaches Jellyfin at another address (from another container or host), use that one.
Replace API key with a key from Dashboard > API Keys.
Leave JSON Payload as it is, and select the notification types Request Approved and Request Automatically Approved. A series request adds the whole series. If the requester has libraries of their own on the Users tab, the title goes there.

Catalogs

Turn on a catalog to import its titles into your library. Series get their seasons and episodes after the import, so a big catalog takes a while.

Titles that aren't released yet are imported but stay hidden while Hide titles that aren't released yet is on (Search tab).

Addon search

Jellyfin's search also asks AIOStreams, and you can open and play what it finds right away. To keep a user's searches to the library, use the Users tab.

Search also finds titles that aren't in your library yet. Off: search stays in your library, for every user, and Gelato asks neither AIOStreams nor TMDB.
An actor's or director's page also lists their other movies and series from TMDB. They open like search results. Works only while the search above is on.
"More like this" on a movie or series starts with TMDB's recommendations for it, and the Suggestions tab of Movies adds TMDB's picks to each row: up to half of them titles you don't have yet. They open like search results. A title or row seen for the first time may only show them the next time. Works only while the search above is on.
A search result you open stays out of the library, the home screen rows, and the counts until someone plays or keeps it (favorite, watchlist, collection, or playlist). Search still finds it. Gelato removes it after 5 days if nobody does.

Search bar toggle

A globe button in the search bar switches a search between the addon and your library only. Needs the JavaScript Injector plugin, installed and set up.
The JavaScript Injector plugin isn't installed: install it from the plugin catalog to use this.

Unreleased titles

Keeps titles that aren't released yet out of search, and hides Gelato's titles in your libraries until they are.

A title counts as released this many days after its release date. 0: on the day itself.

Streams

Checks a stream's tracks when you open a title's page or pick another version, and the next episode's stream while an episode is ending, so playback starts without that wait.
A title's page fills in the video, audio and subtitle lists once the check above has read them, a few seconds after it opens, without opening the page again. In Jellyfin's web app, with the JavaScript Injector plugin.
The JavaScript Injector plugin isn't installed: install it from the plugin catalog to use this.
Skips a stream known not to work (a dead link, or one with no video) and plays the title's next stream, trying the first 3. A version you pick yourself plays as it is.
How long Gelato keeps a title's streams before it asks the addon again. Default: 3600 (1 hour).

RemuxDB

Shows a stream's audio, subtitles, and quality before you play it, and playback starts faster.
Shares that info for streams you play that RemuxDB doesn't know yet, so others see it too. Anonymous: no links, accounts, or personal data.

FFmpeg

How much of a stream FFmpeg reads to find its tracks. Change these only if playback misses tracks or starts slowly.

Default: 5M.
Default: 40M.
